## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Footer supports dark mode
The system SHALL ensure the footer renders correctly in dark mode with appropriate contrast.

#### Scenario: Footer in dark mode
- **WHEN** dark mode is active
- **THEN** the footer background SHALL match the page background
- **AND** text colors SHALL be readable
- **AND** links SHALL have appropriate hover states

#### Scenario: Footer icons in dark mode
- **WHEN** dark mode is active
- **THEN** social media icons SHALL be visible
- **AND** brand elements SHALL maintain contrast
